    Choose your companion

      Start with at least three connected friends. Five or six players use the extended island and paired turns. There are no bots. Disconnected seats pause the table.

      Your island guide

      Reach 10 points on your own opportunity to win. Settlements are 1 point, cities 2; Longest Route and Largest Army are 2 each. Your hidden victory-point cards also count.

      Variable setup places two settlements with adjacent roads in forward then reverse order. Your second settlement supplies your initial resources. Beginner setup uses the published board and placements.

      Roll first, then trade and build in any order. On a 7, everyone holding more than seven resource cards returns half (rounded down); then move the robber and choose a neighboring player to rob. The robber blocks production on its tile.

      With five or six players, Player 1 rolls and acts, then Player 2 (three seats clockwise) acts without player-to-player trades. Both markers then advance clockwise. Each opportunity refreshes development-card eligibility.

      Road: wood + brick. Settlement: wood + brick + wool + wheat, connected to your roads and at least two edges from any building. City: replace your settlement for 3 ore + 2 wheat; the settlement returns to your supply. Development: ore + wool + wheat.

      Bank trades cost 4 of one resource for 1 other, improved by 3:1 general ports or a matching 2:1 port. No gifts or trades that exchange the same resource. Interested players accept an offer; its active player confirms the partner.

      Play one older development card per opportunity, before rolling or during actions: Knight moves the robber; Monopoly takes one resource type from everyone; Invention / Year of Plenty takes two bank resources; Road Building places two free legal roads. VP cards are automatically revealed when you win.

      Longest Route / Longest Road needs at least five connected roads without repeating an edge; another player's building interrupts it. Largest Army needs three played Knights. Ties retain the current award holder.

      Choose a build type, select a highlighted location or use the keyboard location list, then confirm. Zoom and drag the island as needed. Motion and sound have independent controls.
