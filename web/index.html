Make a private table for friends.

    Start with at least two friends connected. Leave room transfers hosting to a connected friend. Keep this tab open to recover from a lost connection.

    A little friendly competition.

    2–8 players. Seven cards each. Empty your hand to win.

    Match the active color, number or symbol. You may always draw one instead; drawing ends your turn.

    ⊘ Skip skips the next player. ⇄ Reverse reverses direction, or skips the other player at a two-seat table.

    +2 answers +2 in any color. Wild +4 answers either +2 or +4. A +2 cannot answer +4. Stack or draw the full accumulated penalty and lose your turn.

    Both Wilds let you choose a color; +4 is always legal outside a penalty stack. No challenges. A final Wild needs its color selected before you win.

    UNO! With two cards, arm UNO before playing. If you forget and leave one card, any opponent can catch you for two cards until the next accepted play or draw. Wild catches open after color selection.

    Old discards refill an empty stock, keeping the top card. If a penalty exceeds available cards, draw what remains. A full rotation of empty-stock passes is a draw.

    Use Tab and Enter/Space. Scroll your hand horizontally. Bots use their own hand and public information, choose the lowest legal card ID, call UNO and catch missed calls. No scoring between rounds.
